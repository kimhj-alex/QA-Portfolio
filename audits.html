Methodology

"Structured sessions of real use — not scripted tests on demo environments."

Each audit began as ordinary use of a platform I was actively working with. When friction appeared, I documented it — steps to reproduce, expected vs actual behavior, severity rationale, and suggested fixes. The audit format structures those observations into a coherent report without losing the authenticity of how the findings were made.
