Form Submissions Thread into a Single Gmail Conversation with No Documented Fix

Low Severity Web Email Integration Hidden Workaround
ID BR-004
Severity Low
Priority Low
Status Found Apr 2026
Platform Squarespace · Gmail
Environment macOS · Brave · squarespace.com · gmail.com
Feature Area Forms · Email Notifications · Gmail Integration
Tester Hyeji Kim (Alex)
Date 2026

Summary

When a Squarespace contact form is configured to send submissions to a Gmail inbox, all submissions from the same form arrive with an identical subject line — "Form Submission: [form name]" — causing Gmail's conversation threading to group every customer inquiry into a single thread. For business owners managing customer communications, this means all inquiries are stacked inside one conversation, making it impossible to track or respond to individual customers without scrolling through an ever-growing thread. Squarespace provides no documentation or in-product guidance on this behavior. The only effective workaround — adding a Subject input field to the form so that each customer's response generates a unique subject line — is documented exclusively in community forums, not in official Squarespace support materials. An alternative workaround of disabling Gmail's conversation view resolves the threading but fundamentally changes the inbox experience in a way that is unsuitable for most users.

Steps to Reproduce

  1. 1.Create a contact form in Squarespace and configure it to send submissions to a Gmail address
  2. 2.Submit the form multiple times with different names and inquiry content
  3. 3.Open Gmail and navigate to the inbox
  4. 4.Observe: all submissions appear as a single thread titled "Form Submission: [form name]"
  5. 5.Attempt to find documentation in Squarespace's help center explaining this behavior or how to resolve it
  6. 6.Observe: no official documentation addresses this issue
  7. 7.Search community forums — workaround discovered: add a Subject input field to the form
  8. 8.Add Subject field to form, resubmit — observe each submission now generates a unique subject line and appears as an individual thread in Gmail

Expected Result

Squarespace form submissions arrive in Gmail as individual emails, or Squarespace's documentation clearly explains the threading behavior and provides guidance on how to configure forms to avoid it — including the Subject field workaround.

Actual Result

All submissions from the same form thread into a single Gmail conversation. No official documentation explains this behavior or the workaround. The fix is only discoverable through community forums and external searching.

The Threading Problem

Gmail groups emails with identical subject lines into one conversation. Without a subject field, every form submission looks like this:

Form Submission: Contact Us 47 messages

With the Subject field workaround, each submission becomes its own thread:

Form Submission: Contact Us — Tattoo booking inquiry 1 message
Form Submission: Contact Us — Question about pricing 1 message
Form Submission: Contact Us — Appointment for March 1 message

Severity Rationale

Why Low

The workaround is effective and easy to implement once discovered. The issue is a documentation and discoverability failure — not a functional break. Severity would increase for businesses that cannot add a subject field due to form design constraints or customer experience considerations.

Suggested Fix

Squarespace should document the Gmail threading behavior in its form settings help content and proactively recommend adding a Subject field when Gmail is selected as the notification destination. Ideally, Squarespace generates a unique identifier per submission to append to the subject line automatically, removing the need for a customer-facing Subject field entirely.
