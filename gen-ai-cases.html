Gen AI Evaluation

Gen AI
Cases 3 case studies · a year of daily use

Narrative write-ups of specific moments where an AI assistant's output sounded right and wasn't — found through a year of hands-on Gen AI use across coding, creative writing, and business planning. Built toward CT-GenAI, following CTFL.

"The goal isn't to prove AI gets things wrong — it's to show what it takes to notice exactly how, and exactly when it matters."

Unlike the audits, these aren't structured test sessions with a checklist. They're retrospectives on real projects — an app, two published novellas, a small business — built alongside AI over the past year, with the specific moments where its output needed a second look pulled out and examined. Each case traces its finding to something checkable: shipped code, a published book, or a live site.

Methodology

"Every finding here comes from real use — not a benchmark, not a red-team prompt, just paying close attention."

Each case study started as an ordinary AI-assisted session — building an app, drafting a novel, planning a business — not a deliberate attempt to break anything. The findings surfaced because the output was checked against what was actually true: the working reference file, the established story detail, the previously agreed number. Every fix described is verified against something outside the conversation itself — shipped code, a published book, or a live site — rather than resting on the conversation log alone. More case studies are added as they accumulate through ongoing daily use.
