Instagram

Ad Pricing Information Incomplete on Mobile Compared to Desktop

High Severity Ads · Pricing Cross-Platform iOS
ID BR-001
Severity High
Priority High
Status Open
Platform Instagram
Environment iPhone · iOS · Instagram app · macOS · Brave · instagram.com
Feature Area Ads · Pricing · Cross-platform
Tester Hyeji Kim (Alex)
Date 2026
↓ Download PDF

Summary

When setting up a boosted post through the Instagram iOS app, the Apple service fee — a significant additional charge on top of the advertised ad budget — is not disclosed as a concrete amount at any point during the setup flow. The fee appears as "To be calculated" on the Budget & duration screen and is absent entirely from the Review screen. The full total including fees is only revealed at the final Payment step, where an ad budget of ₩73,388 becomes a total charge of ₩106,092 — an increase of approximately 45% due to Apple service fees (₩28,934) and estimated taxes (₩9,645). By contrast, the desktop version of the same ad setup shows zero additional fees and explicitly informs the user they are avoiding the Apple service fee by boosting on the web. The mobile flow withholds the true cost until the final step and does not prompt users to consider completing the flow on desktop where the fee does not apply.

Steps to Reproduce

  1. 1.Open the Instagram app on iOS and navigate to a post eligible for boosting
  2. 2.Tap Boost post and proceed through the ad setup flow
  3. 3.On the Budget & duration screen — observe Apple service fee is listed as "To be calculated" with no estimated amount
  4. 4.Proceed to the Review screen — observe no Apple service fee or total including fees is displayed
  5. 5.Tap Add funds and proceed to the Payment screen
  6. 6.Observe: the true total is now shown for the first time — ₩106,092 vs the advertised ad budget of ₩73,388
  7. 7.Abandon the mobile flow and repeat the same ad setup on desktop at instagram.com
  8. 8.Observe: the Payment summary shows ₩0 in additional fees and explicitly states the Apple service fee is being avoided by boosting on the web

Expected Result

The Apple service fee is disclosed as a concrete estimated amount early in the mobile ad setup flow — at minimum on the Budget & duration screen — so users can make an informed decision before reaching the final payment step. Users are also informed at the start of the mobile flow that completing the setup on desktop avoids the fee entirely.

Actual Result

The Apple service fee is shown only as "To be calculated" throughout the mobile setup flow and does not appear as a concrete amount until the final Payment screen. The desktop alternative — which carries no additional fees — is not surfaced during the mobile flow. Users who complete the process on mobile without prior knowledge of the fee structure are presented with a total approximately 45% higher than the ad budget they selected, only at the point of payment.

Severity Rationale

Why High

A nearly 45% cost increase disclosed only at the final payment step constitutes a significant pricing transparency failure. Users who set a budget expecting to pay ₩73,388 are presented with a ₩106,092 charge with no prior warning. The desktop flow avoids this entirely — meaning the issue is platform-specific and correctable.

Key Discrepancy

Mobile vs Desktop

Mobile (iOS app)
Ad budget: ₩73,388
Apple service fee: ₩28,934
Estimated taxes: ₩9,645
Total: ₩106,092

Desktop (instagram.com)

Ad budget: ₩73,388
Estimated tax: ₩0
Total: ₩73,388

Desktop explicitly states: "You're avoiding an Apple service fee by boosting on the web."

Suggested Fix

Display an estimated Apple service fee on the Budget & duration screen as soon as a budget is entered. Add a prompt early in the mobile flow informing users that boosting on desktop avoids the Apple service fee entirely.
