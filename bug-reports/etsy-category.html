Etsy

No Category Exists for Wireless Earbud Cases — Sellers Forced to Misclassify

Low Severity Web Taxonomy Seller Experience
ID BR-005
Severity Low
Priority Low
Status Open
Platform Etsy
Environment macOS · Brave · etsy.com · Seller Dashboard
Feature Area Listing Creation · Product Category
Tester Hyeji Kim (Alex)
Date 2026

Summary

When creating a product listing on Etsy for a wireless earbud case — one of the most commonly sold accessories in the print-on-demand and handmade markets — no accurate category exists in the category selector. Searching for "earbuds," "AirPods," or "wireless earbud case" returns no matching category. The closest available options are Headphones, Phone Cases, or Gadgets — none of which accurately describes the product. Category is a mandatory field marked with an asterisk; sellers cannot leave it blank or submit a listing without selecting one. This forces every seller of earbud cases into misclassifying their product, which affects search discoverability for both sellers and buyers. Wireless earbuds have been mainstream since 2016, making the absence of a dedicated category a significant gap in Etsy's product taxonomy for an entire product type with high market demand.

Steps to Reproduce

  1. 1.Log in to Etsy as a seller and navigate to Listings → Add a listing
  2. 2.Scroll to the Category field — note it is marked as mandatory with an asterisk
  3. 3.Type "earbud" in the category search box
  4. 4.Observe: no matching category appears in the dropdown suggestions
  5. 5.Type "AirPods" — observe: no matching category
  6. 6.Type "wireless" — observe: no matching category specific to earbuds or earbud cases
  7. 7.Attempt to submit the listing without selecting a category
  8. 8.Observe: submission is blocked — category is required
  9. 9.Select the closest available option — Headphones — to proceed

Expected Result

Etsy's category taxonomy includes a dedicated category for wireless earbud cases — or at minimum a subcategory under Electronics Accessories that accurately covers the product type. Sellers can classify their products accurately, which in turn improves search discoverability for buyers.

Actual Result

No category exists for wireless earbud cases. The category field is mandatory, forcing sellers to select an inaccurate category — most commonly Headphones — to complete their listing. This misclassification affects how the product appears in search results and category browsing for buyers.

Available vs Missing

What sellers can choose from:

  • Headphones
  • Phone Cases
  • Gadgets
  • Earbud / AirPods Cases — missing

Severity Rationale

Why Low

Sellers can still list and sell their products by selecting an imperfect category. The issue is a taxonomy gap rather than a functional failure — but it has real downstream effects on search discoverability for both sellers and buyers browsing by category.

Impact on Discoverability

A buyer browsing the Headphones category is not looking for earbud cases. A seller listing there gets visibility in the wrong audience. Both sides of the marketplace are affected by a taxonomy that hasn't kept pace with mainstream consumer electronics.

Suggested Fix

Add a dedicated subcategory for wireless earbud and AirPods cases under Electronics Accessories. Alternatively, allow sellers to suggest missing categories during listing creation, with a review and approval workflow to keep taxonomy current with market trends.
